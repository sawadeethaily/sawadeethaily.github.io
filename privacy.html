Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-05-06

SawadeeThaily ("we", "our", "the channel") is a personal news-curation channel that translates Thai news for Korean readers and publishes content to Instagram, Facebook, and TikTok. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle data.

1. Data we collect

We do not collect any personal data from end users. SawadeeThaily is a one-way publishing channel: we curate news and publish posts. We do not run a website that accepts user input, and we do not store user accounts.

2. Source data

News content is sourced from publicly available RSS feeds of Thai news outlets. Source links are credited in our posts where applicable.

3. Third-party services

To publish content, we use the official APIs of Meta (Instagram Graph API, Facebook Graph API) and TikTok Content Posting API. Authentication uses OAuth tokens issued to our channel accounts. No end-user data is exchanged with these APIs.

4. Cookies / tracking

This page (and the SawadeeThaily GitHub Pages site) uses no cookies and no tracking scripts.

5. Children's privacy

Our service is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

6. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the date at the top.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy: sawadeethaily@gmail.com
