Data Deletion Request

Last updated: 2026-05-06

Summary: SawadeeThaily does not collect, store, or process personal data from end users. There is therefore no user data on our side that requires a deletion request.

How to request deletion

If you believe content on our channel includes personal information about you (for example a photograph in which you are recognizable, or text that names you), you may request deletion of that specific post.

Contact

Send your request to sawadeethaily@gmail.com with:

  • The post URL or screenshot
  • A brief description of why deletion is requested

Response time

We aim to acknowledge requests within 7 days and to action valid requests within 30 days.

Meta / Instagram / Facebook user data

Our channel uses Meta's Graph APIs to publish posts to our own channel accounts. We do not request, receive, or store data of other Meta users. There is no Meta-platform user data on our side to delete.
