# SawadeeThaily

**Thai news, curated and translated for Korean-speaking readers.**

SawadeeThaily is a personal, non-commercial news-curation channel. We summarize Thai news in Korean and publish daily card-style digests to Instagram, Facebook, and TikTok.

---

## What we do

- **Curate** — review Thai RSS feeds daily for stories relevant to Korean travelers, residents, and people interested in Thailand
- **Translate & summarize** — produce concise Korean summaries with the original headline and source clearly credited
- **Publish** — distribute card-style digests (image carousels and short videos) across Instagram, Facebook, and TikTok
- **Protect privacy** — automatically blur faces of bystanders / non-public figures in news photos

## Where to find us

## How TikTok publishing works (Content Posting API)

We use TikTok's official Content Posting API to upload short news videos to our own channel `@sawadeethaily`. Scope used:

- `video.publish` — to post the daily news digest videos to our own account
- `user.info.basic` — to identify the authenticated channel account

We do not post on behalf of any other user. All content is produced and reviewed by the channel operator before publication.

## Sample output

- A daily 5-news digest (table of contents card + 5 news cards + 1–2 food/place recommendation cards)
- Cards are 1080×1080 PNG/JPG and 60–120 second narrated videos
